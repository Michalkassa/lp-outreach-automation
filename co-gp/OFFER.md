## The partnership

- **Manage together.** Valori is the investment manager. A Co-GP works alongside
  us on the fund, not underneath us as a distributor.
- **Governance and rights are open.** We are flexible on both and expect to
  negotiate them rather than present them.

## The capital match

- We have **€50M committed** today, against a **€400M target** fund.
- **Match scheme: €100M + €50M.** If the partner brings €100M, a further €50M
  follows.
- **[CONFIRM]** Who provides the additional €50M, and what triggers it. As
  written this reads two ways: the existing anchor matching a partner's raise,
  or the partner matching in two tranches. An email cannot describe a capital
  structure ambiguously, so this needs settling before drafting.

For scale: **€100M is 25% of the €400M target.** That is exactly the average
anchor stake in a first-time fund and the top of the usual 10–33% seeder band,
so the ask is in market rather than unusual.

## The economics

- **Half management fees and carry.**
  **[CONFIRM]** Half the headline rate, or half of Valori's share? A 50% break
  off a standard 2/20 lands near the 1–1.5% fee and 10–15% carry that anchors
  typically negotiate, which is credible either way, but the two readings are
  different deals.
- **An additional 20% of the total carry pool** on top.
  This sits inside the normal 15–25% management-company revenue share for a seed
  or anchor arrangement.

## Co-investment rights

- **First look** on co-investment opportunities.
- **First priority allocation** alongside the first anchor, **pro rata**.

## Expected return

- **17.4% IRR expected.**
- **[CONFIRM]** Gross or net, and to whom. The LP programme quotes **15%+ net
  IRR to LPs** and **19% IRR** on the €1.35B previously underwritten. A third
  number in the same house needs a clear basis, especially since a Co-GP's
  return is lifted by the fee and carry break and so is genuinely not the LP
  number. Do not put 17.4% in an email beside the LP figures until this is
  pinned down.
