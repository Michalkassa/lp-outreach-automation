# CAPTION — Subject-line rules, Co-GP programme

## Format (one structure, never changes)

    Valori Capital, [what], [rotating ending]

The LP format is kept because the rotation discipline is what stops a batch
looking mass-sent. The vocabulary is not kept: LP category labels and net-IRR
endings are fund marketing and read wrong to a principal being offered a
partnership.

## [what] — the proposition, not a product category

Pick the one the body actually leads on:

- `co-GP partnership`
- `European real estate credit, co-GP`
- `partnership on a €400M credit fund`

## [ending] — rotate across a batch, never twice in a row

Terms carry further than performance here, because the terms are the offer.

**Terms:**
- `€100M matched`
- `half fees and carry`
- `20% of the carry pool`
- `first look on co-investments`

**Scale:**
- `€400M target, €50M committed`

## Do not use

- **Any expected-return figure**, including 17.4%, until its basis is confirmed
  in `co-gp/OFFER.md`. A return number in a subject line is the least
  defensible place to be imprecise.
- LP category labels: "niche European private credit strategies", "European real
  asset debt", "special situations credit". Those describe a product to an
  allocator.
- `first vintage` — retired across both programmes.

## Rules

- Format fixed, only the ending rotates.
- Never repeat an ending on two consecutive prospects in a batch.
- Never put a term in the subject the body does not support. If the body does not
  mention the carry pool, the subject cannot either.
- Every figure matches `co-gp/OFFER.md` exactly.
- Aim under ~70 characters.

## Examples

- Valori Capital, co-GP partnership, €100M matched
- Valori Capital, European real estate credit, co-GP, half fees and carry
- Valori Capital, co-GP partnership, first look on co-investments
- Valori Capital, partnership on a €400M credit fund, 20% of the carry pool
