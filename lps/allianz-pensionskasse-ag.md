## Investment Strategy
The Pensionskasse invests along strict ESG lines: fossil fuel and UN Global Compact exclusions across asset classes "particularly in real estate and infrastructure," with a stated push toward "products explicitly labelled as impact funds or as type 8 or 9 of the SFDR" (Csurda, IPE Aug 2023). Confirmed alternatives are real-asset EQUITY: real estate holdings and an impact infrastructure fund committed at the beginning of 2023 — no locally confirmed private credit. The natural route to credit exists at group level: AllianzGI manages €30B of private credit (€9B European) and its Core Private Markets Fund bundles corporate private debt and infrastructure debt for DACH pension investors (IPE). It offers all five Austrian investment styles per OeKB measurement (WKO Vorsorgereport). H1 2023 performance ranged +1.1% to +3.2% by strategy.
