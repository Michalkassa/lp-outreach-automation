## Investment Strategy
ALTUM is Latvia's state development-finance institution. It acts as an LP — 16 VC funds plus the EIF-managed Baltic Innovation Funds, and a fresh EUR 20M into the 1 Asset Management Baltic Capital Markets Acceleration Fund (financing EUR 5-100M Baltic companies preparing for IPOs). All of it is Baltic SME / capital-markets development: venture, growth, pre-IPO equity. There is no private-credit-backed-by-real-estate line, and cheque sizes are modest. Genuine fund-LP behaviour, but wrong strategy and small scale.
