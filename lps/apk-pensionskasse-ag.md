## Investment Strategy
APK invests "indirectly and primarily through closed-end funds in private equity, hedge funds, private debt, infrastructure and real estate to make its portfolio more resilient to stress" (SWF Institute). Alternatives are 12% of the portfolio, with private equity the largest bucket: "We view private equity as a replacement for, or diversification of, public equity" — Michael Bujatti, senior investment manager for alternatives (IPE, Oct 2025). On private debt they are deliberate rather than absent: cautious on senior direct lending, but they expect "interesting opportunities... in private debt in the next two to four years with restrictive lending conditions" and "could set a new course in this segment in the coming months" (IPE, Oct 2025). In 2025 APK shifted exposure from US dollar assets toward Europe, favoring European credit (IPE). Sector context: Austrian pension alternatives average 26.1% of assets with private debt +31.7% y/y (FMA via IPE, Oct 2025). APK runs its own sustainable investment concept and is a PRI signatory.
