## Investment Strategy
The Bundespensionskasse invests under the "allgemeinen Vorsichtsprinzip" so that "das Vermögen zum größtmöglichen Nutzen der Berechtigten veranlagt wird," mostly via a Spezialfonds it manages itself (bundespensionskasse.at). The published strategic mix (April 2026) is diversified but credit-free on the private side: 24% equities, 30% bonds across three sleeves, 15% real estate & infrastructure, 20% renewable energy & resources, 10% commodities. That 35% real-asset block is equity exposure — no private debt, direct lending, or credit fund line appears anywhere in the disclosed mix. There is no minimum return guarantee, and investments must weigh "ethischen, ökologischen und/oder sozialen Kriterien." As a state-owned vehicle for civil servants it publishes full governance and business reports annually.
