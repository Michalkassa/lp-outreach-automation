## Investment Strategy
CDP is Italy's promotional institution and quasi-SWF. Since 2019 CDP Equity commits to private equity, private debt, VC and real-asset funds — but explicitly "mainly managed by asset management companies part of CDP Group" and aimed at building the *Italian* private-capital ecosystem. CDP Real Asset SGR does real estate, but as domestic equity/infrastructure closed-end funds. Its 3-year plan deploys >USD 4B of own funds to crowd in third parties — all domestically oriented. A pan-European distressed-RE-credit fund with an Italian sleeve *might* be a conversation, but only if framed to Italian-ecosystem policy goals, which is not Valori's pitch.
