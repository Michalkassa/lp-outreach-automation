## Investment Strategy
Česká spořitelna penzijní operates five participant funds (Conservative, Balanced, Dynamic, Ethical, and the new alternative "Fond pro budoucnost") plus one legacy Transformed fund for pre-2013 contracts. The Balanced fund returned 10.93% in 2025; the Dynamic fund returned 17.01% in 2025 — in line with the Czech industry average of 16.5% for dynamic funds. The 2024 Czech pension reform (effective 1 Jan 2025) created a new "alternative participant fund" category allowing pension companies to invest in private equity, real estate, infrastructure, and other illiquid assets for the first time. ČSPS was one of only two companies (alongside Generali) to launch such a fund. "Fond pro budoucnost" (Fund for the Future) received its CNB license and opened to the public in January 2025. It targets housing, healthcare, energy, and local Czech investments, with allocation across equities, bonds, and alternative investments. During its ramp-up phase in 2025, the fund was primarily deployed in money-market instruments while gathering assets. A statute amendment approved by CNB on 1 April 2025 increased investment limits for collective investment funds and loans, broadening the fund's deployment capacity. Fee structure: 0% management and performance fees waived for 2025-2026; from 2027 onwards, 1% management fee + 25% performance fee. Target audience is younger savers with long investment horizons.

## People & Org Structure
Libor Vošický became Chairman of the Board / CEO in January 2026, succeeding Aleš Poklop who departed after 33 years with the Česká spořitelna group. Vošický studied at Czech Technical University (electrical engineering), has been with the ČS group since 2006 (previously Commerzbank and Citibank), and simultaneously leads Stavební spořitelna České spořitelny. He was elected Vice-President of the Association of Pension Companies of the Czech Republic. Jakub Malík serves as Investment Manager / Pension Fund Manager, responsible for fund portfolio management and has publicly spoken on residential real estate as a portfolio component and defense-sector investments. Investment decisions are made locally in Prague but within the Erste Group governance framework. Erste Asset Management (Vienna) provides ESG and responsible-investment support.
