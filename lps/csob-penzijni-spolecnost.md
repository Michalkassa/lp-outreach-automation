## Investment Strategy
ČSOB PS manages the standard Czech third-pillar lineup — guaranteed transformed fund plus conservative, balanced (vyvážený), dynamic, and responsible participation funds (csob-penze.cz). The 2024 Czech reform is the strategic fact: from 1 January 2024, pension companies may launch alternative participant funds "opening doors to non-traditional investments" (penize.cz), and competitors have moved — Generali PS (Jan 2025), ČS PS "Fond budoucnosti" (Jan 2025), and Rentea (May 2025) all launched alternative funds. ČSOB has not visibly launched one [VERIFY], leaving it behind the market on exactly the vehicle that could hold a Valori-type commitment. Parent KBC's group asset management supports the platform.
