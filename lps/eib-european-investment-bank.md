## Investment Strategy
EIF is the single most active fund-of-funds LP in Europe and, uniquely among this batch, explicitly anchors *first-time* managers and commits to *private credit* — both exactly what Valori would want in an LP. The catch is mandate. EIF deploys under InvestEU and EU access-to-finance objectives: venture, growth equity, and SME/mid-cap private debt. Its private-credit commitments (e.g. the Sienna defence-SME fund) finance SMEs for policy goals, not opportunistic distressed real-estate credit for return. There is no evidence EIF backs commercial RE-distressed strategies, and its process is policy-gated and slow. Strong LP behaviour, wrong mandate lane.
