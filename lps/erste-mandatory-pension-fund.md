## Investment Strategy
Erste Plavi runs Croatia's fastest-growing mandatory funds: Category A membership +32.3% in 2024, A and B returns above 12%, and B holding a 17.66% member share (mirovina.hr). Chairman Petar Vlaić attributes performance partly to "the liberalization of investments" and is public about direction: foreign investments at 35% and "rising to 50 percent in the coming years," plus "positive experience from real estate investments, including the purchase of a shopping center in Koper, Slovenia, with an expected return above 11%" (mirovina.hr). That is a mandatory Pillar II fund doing cross-border direct real estate — equity, not credit, which under the rubric is the wrong bucket, and the Pillar II blocker caps the row regardless. If the blocker is relaxed, Erste Plavi's appetite for illiquid real assets and its CFA-led, liberalization-minded leadership make it the most engaged prospective Croatian mandatory conversation.
