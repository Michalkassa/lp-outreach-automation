## Investment Strategy
Polish OFE context (sourced): open pension funds closed 2025 with a record 42.1% average return and assets of PLN 293.5B (~€68B) (interia.pl). But the mandate is a citable blocker under the rubric: OFE operate under a statutorily "limited catalogue of permissible investments" (rankingofe.com.pl) — heavy mandatory listed-equity share, no treasury bonds, and no room for closed-end private fund commitments (rp.pl argues OFE *should* be allowed to invest in VC/PE funds, confirming they currently cannot). The "suwak" mechanism also transfers members' assets to ZUS as retirement approaches, and the OFE→IKE conversion reform remains suspended — a system in slow structural wind-down. Until the investment catalogue is widened by statute, no OFE can hold a Valori-type fund. Mid-size OFE inside the Generali group family.
