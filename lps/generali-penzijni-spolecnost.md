## Summary
Generali penzijní společnost is the first Czech pension company to launch an alternative participant fund ("Future"), with €5.4B AUM and nearly 1 million clients. The 50% alternatives allocation in the new fund is a confirmed and active mandate. Generali Group (parent) has its own alternatives capabilities globally. Bridge: Generali penzijní was first mover in Czech alternative pension funds (2024 launch), demonstrating genuine appetite for illiquid real assets beyond public markets.
