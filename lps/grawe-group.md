## Investment Strategy
GRAWE is not just an insurer: the Graz group unites 20 insurance companies across 13 CEE/SEE countries, the GRAWE Banking Group (Bank Burgenland, Schelhammer Capital, DADAT, Security KAG, GBG Private Markets), and one of Austria's largest real estate portfolios (grawe.at group pages). Its stated investment philosophy is security through real assets: GRAWE "responded to the economic conditions... by investing in real estate and real property in order to guarantee the most important asset of any insurance firm: security" (grawe.at history). 2024: group balance sheet €14.46B (+11.6%), AG premiums €966.2M (+9.4%), EGT €69.6M (−14.8%, weather losses; ORF Steiermark). The 2024 report is literally titled "UNSERE WERTE BESTIMMEN UNSEREN WEG" (our values determine our path).
