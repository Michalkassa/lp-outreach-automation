## Investment Strategy
GRAWE allocates approximately 25% of its investment portfolio to real estate and 75% to securities and participations. Real estate has historically held a central position in GRAWE's asset strategy, with the company pursuing a "buy and hold" philosophy -- acquiring, renovating and managing properties for long-term value creation. The dedicated real estate arm, GRAWE Immobiliengruppe, is consolidated under GRAWE IMMO AG and covers portfolio management, property development, construction management, site development, financing, marketing and rental across Austria and Southeast Europe. The portfolio includes historical buildings in prime urban locations, modern office buildings, shopping centres and residential complexes, focused on Graz, Vienna, Klagenfurt and Leoben domestically, with Southeast European holdings through subsidiaries. In October 2025 GRAWE acquired four prime properties in Graz city centre (Herrengasse 7, 9 and 22-26, Am Eisernen Tor 3) from Generali Versicherung, reinforcing its position as a leading Austrian real estate group. The group also operates a banking division with asset management capabilities through Security KAG and Capital Bank. GRAWE's investment philosophy emphasises sustainability, preservation of value, and long-term orientation consistent with its mutual ownership structure -- there are no external shareholders demanding short-term returns.

## Summary
Grazer Wechselseitige Versicherung AG (GRAWE) is a 198-year-old Austrian mutual insurer with EUR 2.19B+ in equity, EUR 1.5B+ in annual premiums, and operations across 14 countries. The group allocates approximately 25% of its investment portfolio to real estate through GRAWE Immobiliengruppe, its own dedicated platform for acquisition, development and management of properties across Austria and Southeast Europe.
