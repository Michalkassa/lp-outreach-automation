## Investment Strategy
ICO runs FOND-ICO Global, Spain's first public fund-of-funds (launched 2013, now EUR 4.5B via Axis). It is a genuine LP that backs emerging managers — but its charter is to build the ecosystem of funds "that invest in Spanish companies," and its focus is venture and private equity, not real-estate-backed distressed credit. As of Dec 2025 Axis moved to an open, continuous investment system. The fit gap is double: geography (Spanish portfolio companies) and strategy (VC/PE vs distressed RE credit). A manager would need a substantial Spanish-deployment commitment to be in scope — not Valori's mandate.
