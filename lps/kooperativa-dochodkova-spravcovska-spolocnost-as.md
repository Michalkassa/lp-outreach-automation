## Investment Strategy
Slovak Pillar II context (sector facts, sourced): five DSS operate — Allianz, Kooperativa, NN, Uniqa, VÚB Generali; 1.8M+ savers and €17B+ in pension fund assets, with 70% of savings in non-guaranteed (index/equity/mixed) funds (finreport.sk, 2025). Alternatives are explicitly permitted up to 5% of a fund's NAV, and the DSS association publicly defends them: DSS "already invest part of their assets in alternative investments," noting developed-market pension funds hold 10–30% and citing private equity / venture capital returns (ADSS blog, blog.sme.sk). A 5% sleeve of a multi-billion fund comfortably fits a €10–30M ticket — Pillar II here is an enabled market, not a blocked one. Kooperativa DSS is the sector's newcomer with a deliberately simple three-fund lineup — bond guaranteed, ESG-themed index ("environmentally, socially responsible and transparent companies" — CEO Matej Varga), and a world index fund (kooperativadss.sk). No alternatives exposure exists yet; the fund set is passive by design. The interesting fact is the parent: VIG, whose group book is property-heavy and credit-light (see VIG row) — and whose Slovak pension arm is too small today for a €10M ticket to be prudent.
