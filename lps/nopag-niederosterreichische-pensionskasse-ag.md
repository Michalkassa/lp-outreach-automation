## Investment Strategy
NÖVK manages "Abfertigung Neu" severance capital under the Betriebliches Mitarbeiter- und
Selbständigenvorsorgegesetz (BMSVG), which imposes a **capital guarantee** — the nominal
contributions must be preserved. That regime structurally rules out the illiquid, drawdown,
capital-at-risk profile of a first-vintage distressed-credit fund. NÖVK's public positioning is
sustainability-led (repeatedly ÖGUT-awarded) and it posted a solid 5.64% net return for 2024,
but there is no evidence of private credit, real-asset debt, or alternative-fund commitments —
consistent with the guarantee constraint. This is the same blocker that applies to Vorsorgekassen
across the pipeline.
