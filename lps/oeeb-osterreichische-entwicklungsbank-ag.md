## Investment Strategy
OeEB is the Republic of Austria's development bank and an EDFI member (same family as SIFEM). It provides long-term finance — direct and via PE/debt funds — to private-sector projects in developing and emerging economies, alongside technical assistance. Its mandate is development impact in eligible (non-European-developed) countries. A European real-estate distressed-credit fund is categorically outside that geographic and impact mandate. Not a fit regardless of return profile.
