## Investment Strategy

But the mandate is a citable blocker under the rubric: OFE operate under a statutorily "limited catalogue of permissible investments" (rankingofe.com.pl) — heavy mandatory listed-equity share, no treasury bonds, and no room for closed-end private fund commitments (rp.pl argues OFE *should* be allowed to invest in VC/PE funds, confirming they currently cannot).
