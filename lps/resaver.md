## Investment Strategy
RESAVER is a defined-contribution, cross-border IORP whose investments are run end-to-end by BlackRock as "the sole investment management provider," with Previnet as administrator (IPE, Jun 2023). Member organisations span Austria, Belgium, Cyprus, France, Hungary, Italy, the Netherlands, plus Spain and the Czech Republic operational since 2024 (resaver.eu/OECD STIP). It publishes no AUM figures. A 2023 reorganisation plan foresaw a proper IORP structure with possible new CIO and managing director roles (IPE) — if executed, that would be the first moment in-house allocation decisions (and external managers beyond BlackRock) become plausible. The Horizon Europe 2023–24 work programme funds capacity-building for the fund (CORDIS). No alternatives, private markets, or credit language appears anywhere in its public material.
