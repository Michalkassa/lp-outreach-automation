## Investment Strategy
SIFEM is Switzerland's DFI and a cornerstone of Swiss development cooperation, established 2005 to take over SECO's emerging-market portfolio. It provides long-term finance to private-equity and private-debt funds and financial institutions serving SMEs in developing and emerging economies. Critically, its statute mandates that at least 60% of investment volume go to eligible low-GNI countries (50% for regional/global funds). A European real-estate distressed-credit fund fails that test outright. Even though SIFEM *is* a private-debt fund LP, the geography is a categorical exclusion.
