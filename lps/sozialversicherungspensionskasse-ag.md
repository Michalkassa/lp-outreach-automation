## Investment Strategy
SVPK runs one investment and risk community of ~€506M, invested through mixed funds with management outsourced to Erste Asset Management (svpk.at, Grundsätze der Veranlagung). Stated principles are security, profitability, liquidity, and diversification, overlaid with a hard sustainability screen: "ethical, ecological and social requirements" and portfolio monitoring against the EU Taxonomy Regulation (svpk.at, Veranlagung & Nachhaltigkeit). It publishes a Nachhaltigkeitsbericht and was named an OekoBusiness Vienna company in 2024. Nothing in its public material references alternatives, private markets, real estate, or credit funds — the structure reads as a traditional balanced portfolio delegated to EAM. Sector context (Austrian pension alternatives at 26.1%, FMA via IPE Oct 2025) suggests room, but there is no sign SVPK participates.
