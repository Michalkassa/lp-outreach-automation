## Investment Strategy
Valida runs a liability-driven strategy with a deliberate multi-year build-up of alternatives "to counterbalance an expected reduction in returns from the 'classic' fixed income market" (IPE). With selected managers it has invested in "infrastructure debt, infrastructure equity, private equity, CTAs or CLOs" and was planning a dedicated private debt mandate (IPE, 2020) — real asset debt is already in their vocabulary, though the current allocation needs verification. Roughly 27% sits in "Immobilien und sonstige Investments" (Raiffeisenzeitung). CEO Martin Sardelic: "Our liability-driven investment strategy proved to be resilient in a difficult market environment" (IPE, Feb 2023), with alternatives contributing positively through 2022. The 2024 Pensionskasse result was 8.24% vs. the 7.76% Austrian average (European Pensions, 2025). Sector context: Austrian pension fund alternatives are 26.1% of assets, with private debt growing 31.7% y/y (FMA 2024 report via IPE, Oct 2025) — Valida is a core part of that trend. Sardelic also lobbies publicly for life-phase investment rules allowing higher-yielding allocations for younger savers.
