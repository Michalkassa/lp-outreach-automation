## Investment Strategy
Valida operates a life-phase model ("Lebensphasenmodell") with three strategies: dynamic (expected return 10-17%), balanced (7-12%), and defensive (5-8%). CEO Martin Sardelic has stated that real estate and alternative investments help "become more independent from market or economic cycles" and that Valida has "built up these two investment options in recent years to achieve better diversification" (Raiffeisenzeitung). The fund holds a sizable alternatives sleeve, though at ~21.7% it sits below the Austrian Pensionskassen average of 26.1% (FMA 2024). Across the Austrian sector, private equity grew 55.1% and private debt 31.7% YoY, signalling accelerating appetite for illiquids (IPE, Oct 2025). Valida is a UN PRI signatory.

## People & Org
Martin Sardelic is CEO. Philipp Mayer sits on the board alongside him. Investment decisions are made locally in Vienna. Valida is operationally independent from Raiffeisen Bank International despite the 57.4% ownership.
