## Investment Strategy
As a BVK, VBV-Vorsorgekasse must guarantee 100% of paid-in severance capital, which forces a conservative book: bonds, loans, and held-to-maturity positions at the core, equities and a small private-markets sleeve ("real estate, infrastructure projects, and selected private debt investments," vbv.at) on top. BMSVG rules cap alternatives at roughly 5% of assets. 2025 performance was +4.78% (OeKB) vs. a ~4.7% BVK average in 2024. Whatever private debt appetite exists here is expressed in the low single digits of percent and cannot absorb a €10M illiquid closed-end ticket in any normal course.
