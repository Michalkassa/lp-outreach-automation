## Investment Strategy
VIG runs a conservative, bond-dominated book: 74.6% bonds, 9.6% property, 8.3% cash, 3.4% equities, 2.0% loans across €38B (end-2025 investor deck). Its distinguishing feature is geography — a "market-oriented, decentralised business model" across 20+ CEE countries, with VIG Asset Management based in Hungary "responsible for expanding VIG's investment business" (group.vig). The sustainability programme drives the public investment narrative: "Responsible Investment" approach since 2019 (updated 2023), €1.5B green bonds by 2024, net-zero portfolio ambition by 2050 (group.vig Asset Management page). No private credit, private debt, or alternatives fund program is publicly broken out — the property line (~€3.6B) is the visible real-asset commitment, and the 2% loan book's composition needs verification. With a 261% solvency ratio, capacity is not the constraint; visibility of the asset class is.
