## Investment Strategy
VIG pursues a conservative, security-oriented investment strategy with bonds as the dominant asset class at 74.6% of the €38.0B portfolio (end 2025). The bond book is high quality: 74.5% rated A or above, with government bonds at 57.4%, financials 18.0%, and corporates 16.8%. Property accounts for 9.6% (~€3.6B) and loans for 2.0% (~€760M), providing direct exposure to real estate and credit. Equities represent 3.4%, cash and deposits 8.3%, affiliated companies 1.4%, and alternative investments 0.7% (~€266M). The new Group strategy "evolve28" (2026-2028) focuses on profitable growth, increasing earnings, and a continuously rising dividend while expanding CEE market leadership. Under evolve28, collaboration across the group is being strengthened through the CO3 pillar (Collaboration, Cooperation, Communication). VIG completed the acquisition of Nürnberger Beteiligungs-AG in May 2026 (99.2% stake, €120/share), the largest transaction in its history, significantly expanding its German market presence. Solvency II capital requirements create structural demand for spread-enhancing, capital-efficient allocations such as secured real estate credit -- a natural fit for Valori's mandate.

## People & Org Structure
The Managing Board comprises six members with mandates extended through June 2031. Gerhard Lahner (COO, Second Deputy CEO) oversees Asset Management including Real Estate, Group Treasury & Capital Management, and IT. Liane Hirner serves as Chief Finance and Risk Officer (CFRO), responsible for finance, risk, and accounting. Hartwig Löger as CEO leads overall strategy with country responsibility for Austria, Czechia, and Germany. Peter Höfinger (Deputy Chairman) manages Corporate Business, Reinsurance, and European Affairs. VIG operates on a decentralised "fleet of ships" model: local companies have autonomy while the holding provides strategic direction.
