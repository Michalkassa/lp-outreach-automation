## Investment Strategy
The Sava Insurance Group runs a "risk-aware, return-oriented" investment strategy that complements its underwriting result. The book is heavily bond-weighted — fixed-rate financial investments were 85.7% of a €1,532.1M portfolio at Q1 2024 — with only "selective exposure to equities, investment funds and other asset classes, in line with local regulatory requirements and internal risk limits" (sava-re.si). Higher European rates in 2024 lifted yields on this fixed-income book, and 2024 was a record year: group net profit €87.8M, business volume above €1bn, ROE 13.6%, solvency ~207–213%. The group also owns Sava Infond, Slovenia's largest fund manager (>€900M, ~14% market share) — meaning in-house asset-management capability exists, though no evidence links it to private credit or real asset debt. Nothing in the public disclosures names distressed debt, special situations, private debt, or real estate debt as an allocation. The alternatives fit is therefore plausible-but-unproven: a large, well-capitalised, A+ rated (S&P, June 2025) balance sheet with the capacity to write a €10–50M ticket, but a demonstrably bond-first posture today.
