# CAPTION — email Subject-line rules (governs the Subject on every draft)

## Format (ONE consistent structure — never changes)
    Valori Capital, [category], [ending]

- **[category]** = the LP's `category_label` (from its /lps file / tracker):
  "European real asset debt" | "niche European private credit strategies" | "special situations credit"
- **[ending]** = exactly ONE item, ROTATED across LPs (list below). Either one impressive
  stat OR the strategy descriptor — not both stacked.
- "first vintage" is RETIRED — do not use it as the ending anymore.

## Ending — rotate across LPs (one per subject; never the same one two LPs running)
Mix "results" (stats that impress fund managers) with "strategy" (what we do):

**Results (one stat only):**
- 19% IRR
- 15%+ net IRR
- €1.35B underwritten at 19% IRR   (counts as one ending — the only allowed two-number combo)

**Strategy:**
- private credit backed by European real estate
- real-estate-backed private credit

## Match the body (never contradict it)
Pick the [category] and [ending] that fit what THIS email's body actually says — do not
introduce a term the body does not support.
- **Category follows the body's angle:**
  - body leads on distressed / special situations / turnaround credit -> "special situations credit"
  - body leads on real-estate-backed / downside protection / diversifying beyond bonds -> "European real asset debt"
  - body is general private credit / niche allocation -> "niche European private credit strategies"
- **Ending follows the body's emphasis:**
  - body's hook is the STRATEGY / what we do -> use a strategy-descriptor ending
  - body foregrounds RETURNS or track record -> use a stat ending
- Never put a phrase in the subject the body contradicts (e.g. don't say "alternative
  lending" when the body is about distressed credit; don't headline a stat the body never
  mentions). Rotate for variety, but only within what the body supports.

## Examples
- Valori Capital, European real asset debt, 19% IRR
- Valori Capital, niche European private credit strategies, private credit backed by European real estate
- Valori Capital, special situations credit, 15%+ net IRR
- Valori Capital, European real asset debt, €1.35B underwritten at 19% IRR
- Valori Capital, niche European private credit strategies, real-estate-backed private credit

## Rules
- Format is fixed for every LP; only the **ending** rotates (so subjects vary, not mass-sent).
- One stat per subject (except the "€1.35B underwritten at 19% IRR" combo). Never invent a
  number — every figure matches Fund facts (19% IRR, 15%+ net IRR, €1.35B).
- Keep it short (aim under ~70 characters). No em-dashes, no exclamation marks.
- Category comes from the LP file; if missing, default to "niche European private credit strategies".
- Applies in every language: translate the ending's words, keep the numbers and "Valori Capital".
