# LP Segments — angle and register per investor type

## pension (public/corporate pension funds)
- They care about: yield vs liabilities, downside protection, manager
  track record length, fees, consultant approval.
- Angle: stability + income. Reference their published allocation targets
  (board minutes are public for US publics) and where private credit sits
  vs their target.
- Register: most formal of all segments. Longer sales cycle; the goal of
  email 1 is a call with the private markets team, not enthusiasm.
- Note: many only act through consultants (Meketa, RVK, Albourne...). If
  research shows a consultant gatekeeps, target the consultant relationship
  angle instead and flag it to me.

## insurance
- They care about: capital treatment (rating/NAIC/Solvency II), duration
  match, IG-adjacent private credit, spread over public comps.
- Angle: capital-efficient yield. If our paper is rateable, lead with it.
- Register: technical is fine. They respect specificity on structure.

## endowment_foundation
- They care about: net returns, illiquidity budget, manager quality,
  access to niche/capacity-constrained strategies.
- Angle: differentiation and edge. Why this strategy is hard to replicate.
- Register: intellectual, concise. They read a lot of these emails.

## family_office
- They care about: alignment, co-invest, direct access to partners,
  discretion, tax where relevant.
- Angle: relationship + access. Offer something institutional LPs don't
  get (co-invest rights, direct dialogue with founders).
- Register: warmest and least formal segment. Shorter emails, more human.

## fund_of_funds / OCIO
- They care about: fitting a bucket in THEIR product, track record data,
  ops/DD readiness, capacity.
- Angle: make their job easy. Signal DDQ-ready, data room ready.
- Register: efficient, peer-to-peer. They will judge sloppiness hardest.

## sovereign_wealth
- They care about: scale, co-invest, fee breaks for size, local presence.
- Angle: only pursue if our fund size makes their minimum check viable.
  Flag to me if likely below their minimum — don't waste a touch.
