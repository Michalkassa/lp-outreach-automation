# Voice Examples — Structure v4 (2026-07-21)

The canon is `templates/voice.md`. These are reference emails in the current
voice: a research-grounded fit hook (never opening with the LP's company name),
a mid-motion line with one personal touch, three trust bullets, the deck line
once, and a short confident close. No em-dashes. Body ~120–190 words.

---

## Example 1 — a conservative insurer (protection angle)

Subject: Valori Capital, European real asset debt, first vintage

Dear Mr. [Surname],

I am reaching out because our strategy looks like a natural match for a
conservative insurance balance sheet. We provide secured European real estate
credit, structured for yield with strong downside protection, in tickets of
€10M to €50M.

We are placing the first vintage now, and I am handling these conversations
myself. The target is a €400M close by April 2027, with €50M anchored and €1M
of our own capital in.

- **Track Record:** €1.35B underwritten previously at a 19% IRR, over 50 years of combined experience.
- **Current Dynamics:** €50M committed and a €100M+ proprietary pipeline, mostly bilateral.
- **Target Returns:** 15%+ net IRR to LPs, 15%+ unlevered at the asset level.

Our deck is attached, and every return figure in it is stated net of fees.

If it is worth a look, I would happily walk you through it on a short call.

Kind regards,
Jozef Martinak
Partner, Valori Capital

## What makes these work
- The hook names the fit through a GENERAL framing of the LP's world, drawn from
  their research file. It never quotes their figures or says they asked for
  anything, and never opens with the company name.
- It opens mid-motion (placing / raising / taking shape), with one personal touch.
- No two LPs should share an opener framing, mid-motion verb, personal touch, or
  close. Vary them. It is a flowing style, not a template.
