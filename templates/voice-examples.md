## Extracted voice DNA
- Register: formal European institutional. "Dear Mr./Ms. [Surname]",
  "Kind regards". Full sentences, no fragments, no contractions.
- Length: 200–240 words. Substantial, not chatty.
- Opening move: firm introduction + ONE personalized bridge clause about
  the recipient's firm in sentence two ("Given [Firm]'s focus on
  [their actual mandate], I wanted to introduce our firm as you evaluate
  [category we fit]"). This clause is the ONLY personalized element and
  therefore must be precise and true — it carries the whole email.
- Credibility engine: density of hard numbers. Every claim is quantified
  (fund target + close date, anchor size, GP commit, ticket range, prior
  track record € and IRR, pipeline size, target net IRR). No vague
  adjectives without a number nearby.
- Objection handling: names the weakness itself ("While Valori is a new
  brand...") and answers it immediately with the bullets. Preemptive,
  confident, never defensive.
- Structure (fixed skeleton):
  1. Intro + personalized bridge (2 sentences)
  2. Fund status paragraph (vintage, target, anchor)
  3. Strategy niche paragraph (ticket size, why mega-funds can't access it,
     risk framing)
  4. "While new brand..." pivot + 3 labeled bullets:
     Track Record / Current Dynamics / Target Returns
  5. Values paragraph ("Our Foundation: ...") — Christian values, integrity,
     profit redistribution. Keep; it is a differentiator and a filter.
  6. Close: "Our deck is attached." + CONDITIONAL ask ("If [category]
     aligns with your current mandates or co-investment interests, I would
     welcome a brief introductory call.")
- Ask mechanics: never asks directly for time. Frames the call as
  conditional on THEIR mandate fit — respectful, zero pressure, gives them
  an easy internal justification.
- Punctuation: NO em-dashes or semicolons anywhere. Use commas, full
  stops, or "to" for ranges (€10M to €50M). Labeled bullets stay, they
  are part of the structure.
- Length: 160 to 200 words. The skeleton is what matters, so trim inside
  each section rather than dropping sections.

## What varies per LP vs what stays fixed
FIXED: skeleton, bullets content, values paragraph, sign-off, numbers.
VARIES: (a) the bridge clause — must reflect the LP's real, researched
mandate in their own vocabulary; (b) the category label in the bridge and
in the close ("niche European private credit strategies" ↔ "European real
asset debt" ↔ "special situations credit") — pick whichever matches how
THEY describe their bucket; (c) for family offices, "co-investment
interests" stays in the close; for institutions with no co-invest program,
drop it; (d) salutation formality follows the LP's culture (DACH: strict
"Dear Mr./Ms."; Nordics/UK: first name acceptable after research confirms).

## Draft QA check (run on every draft before showing me)
Fail and rewrite if any are true:
1. The bridge clause could be sent to a different LP unchanged.
2. Any claim about the LP not supported by their /lps/ file.
3. A qualitative superlative with no number within the same sentence
   ("excellent" is allowed only where the winning email uses it).
4. The skeleton order was changed or a section dropped without my
   instruction.
5. Word count outside 160 to 200.
6. The ask is direct ("Are you available Tuesday?") instead of conditional.
7. Numbers differ from CLAUDE.md fund facts (never improvise figures).
8. Tone drifts casual: contractions, fragments, humour, "Hi [First]" for
   a DACH institutional contact.
9. Any em-dash or semicolon anywhere in the email.
10. Two adjectives stacked on one noun ("highly protected, bilateral" from
    the original is the only permitted instance if used verbatim).
11. Any sentence that restates a previous sentence in different words.
12. Perfectly parallel bullet grammar plus identical bullet lengths. Keep
    the three labels, but let the bullets breathe differently.
